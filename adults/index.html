Indoor places for kids

Somewhere to go on a wet day, without waiting for an event. Soft play, swimming, climbing, ninja courses, bowling, cinemas, museums and free library clubs, across Swansea and nearby. Opening hours and prices are copied from each venue's own page; where a venue does not publish them, this says so.

Dated indoor events, like half term workshops and pantos, are in Browse with the Indoors switch on. Hours change in school holidays, so open the venue's page before you go. Know a place that should be here? Use Post an event and send its website.

Post an event

Running a gig, comedy night, class or night out in Swansea, nearby or Cardiff? Email us the details. It goes on the calendar once the date has been checked against a public page.

Email us your event

Opens your email app with a short template to fill in. Or write to ashleykissick1@hotmail.co.uk

Please include

  • The event name
  • The date or dates
  • The time, if it is published
  • The venue and address
  • The cost, if there is one
  • Any age limit, like 18+
  • A link to a public page that shows the date. A Facebook post will not do, it cannot be read.

What happens next

The same rule applies to an emailed event as to one found by our own search.

  1. 1We read your email. Nothing goes on the calendar straight away.
  2. 2The link gets opened. We look for the date you gave on the page you linked.
  3. 3It goes up, or it does not. If the date is there, the event goes up and links back to your page. If not, we may email you back.
  4. 4Blank stays blank. Anything you leave out is shown as not published, never filled in with a guess.

Your email address is only used to reply about your event. It is never published.
